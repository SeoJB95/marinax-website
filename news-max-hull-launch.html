MarinAX to unveil MAX-Hull, its AI-based hull design platform

Join us in Busan for a live demonstration of practical AI for hull-form design, from instant 3D generation to rapid performance prediction and automated CFD workflows.

Event details

Date
Friday, September 18, 2026
Time
11:30–15:00
Venue
Haeundae Centum Hotel
4F, Convention Halls 1 & 2
Busan, Korea

MarinAX is redesigning how ships are designed. The company has transformed experienced naval architects’ judgment, extensive hull and flow data, and engineering expertise into an AI platform that supports decisions in real time.

A practical AI platform for hull-form design

MAX-Hull moves directly from principal hull parameters to complete 3D geometry. Designers can skip slow intermediate fairing and reconstruction steps, explore alternatives, and review performance information within one workflow.

The launch demonstration will cover AI-based hull generation, data-driven design exploration, rapid performance prediction, propulsion performance and Energy Saving Device design, and automated CFD prediction and reporting.

Invitation

MarinAX begins with MAX-Hull, but the broader goal is to build vertical AI for naval architecture. The team believes Korea can lead not only in shipbuilding, but also in the AI systems that shape how future vessels are designed.
