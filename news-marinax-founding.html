A message on the founding of MarinAX Co., Ltd.

We are pleased to announce the founding of MarinAX Co., Ltd.

MarinAX brings together decades of experience in shipbuilding, academic research, and artificial intelligence with one shared vision: to accelerate AI transformation in the maritime industry.

Our first product, MAX-Hull, is an AI-powered hull-form design platform that combines engineering expertise, data, and AI to make the design process faster, smarter, and more efficient.

Our vision extends beyond hull-form design. MarinAX aims to become a vertical AI company specializing in shipbuilding and maritime engineering, expanding into performance prediction, automated optimization, and agentic AI.

Built on industry experience, research, data, and innovation, MarinAX is beginning a new voyage toward the future of maritime AI.

We look forward to seeing you at our product launch on September 18.
